# Home floating image buttons

Replaced the visible KakaoTalk, Instagram, and Website labels and text glyphs with local SVG pictograms. Buttons share a 56px rounded-square silhouette, 12px vertical gaps, restrained shadows, brand colors, and keyboard focus rings. Mobile positioning and reduced-motion preferences are supported. Existing accessible names, dialog targets, and home-only visibility logic are preserved.

Validation: parsed all three SVG assets and checked button image references, accessible labels, and dialog targets. No deployment performed.
