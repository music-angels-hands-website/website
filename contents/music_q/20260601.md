# SHORT FILM REFLECTION

As part of Music Angel Hands, all volunteers are required to complete a short video reflection form.

## Learning Beyond Volunteering

At Music Angel Hands, volunteering is not only about participating in events and activities. We also want our volunteers to have opportunities to learn, reflect, and grow throughout their time with our organization.

---

## Video Reflections

As part of our volunteer program, volunteers watch selected videos that focus on meaningful topics, important messages, and ideas that connect with the values of Music Angel Hands.

After watching each video, volunteers complete a reflection form where they answer questions about what they watched. These reflections allow volunteers to share what stood out to them, explain what they learned, and think more deeply about the message of the video.

---

## Questions Created by Our Leadership

The reflection questions are prepared by our President, Elisabeth, and our Co-President, Joshua.

Each set of questions is created to encourage volunteers to go beyond simply watching the video. Volunteers are given the opportunity to think about the main ideas, consider different perspectives, and connect what they learned to their own experiences and their role in the community.

---

## Reflection & Growth

Through these video reflections, our volunteers are able to develop a better understanding of the values that Music Angel Hands represents. The activities encourage volunteers to be thoughtful, responsible, caring, and aware of the people and communities around them.

Each reflection gives volunteers a chance to express their own ideas and learn something new. Over time, these activities help our members grow not only as volunteers, but also as individuals.

---

## Our Volunteers

Our volunteers are an important part of Music Angel Hands. Through volunteering, music, community involvement, learning activities, and reflection, they continue to build skills and experiences that they can carry with them beyond our organization.

We believe that meaningful service includes both helping others and learning from the experience. Our volunteer reflection program is one of the ways we encourage every member of Music Angel Hands to continue learning, growing, and making a positive difference in their community.
